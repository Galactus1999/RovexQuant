# Strategy catalogue

Strategy families are candidates for research, not guaranteed edges or recommendations. Each implementation must be isolated, versioned, parameterized and tested.

## Trend
SMA/EMA crossovers, MACD, Supertrend, ADX trend-strength filter, Ichimoku.

## Momentum
RSI, stochastic, rate of change, relative strength and sector-relative strength.

## Breakout
Donchian channel, opening-range breakout, volume-confirmed range breakout, volatility contraction breakout.

## Volatility / mean reversion
Bollinger re-entry, ATR regimes/stops, pivot/support-resistance context, pullback-to-trend.

## Intraday
VWAP relationship, opening-range breakout, gap-and-go, intraday pullback. Requires session-aware, quality-checked intraday bars.

## Investment / portfolio
Value, quality, momentum, low-volatility factors, market regime and sector rotation. Later: pairs/statistical arbitrage.

## Validation
Unit tests, walk-forward and out-of-sample evaluation, survivorship/look-ahead/corporate-action audit, costs/slippage/liquidity/circuit simulation, regime/sector/market-cap segmentation. Report expectancy, profit factor, drawdown, turnover, exposure, trade count and tail loss—not win rate alone.
